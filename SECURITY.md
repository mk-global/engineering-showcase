# Security policy

Last verified: 2026-11-07

This repository is documentation and small illustrative examples.
It runs no service and holds no credentials.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find something here that could help someone attack the platform it describes, such as a detail that reveals live configuration, or a weakness in the design that the pages do not already state, please report it privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository: the **Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**.

Please do not open a public issue for it.

I aim to acknowledge reports within five working days.
If the report concerns content in this repository, the fix is a reviewed pull request that removes or corrects it, and I'll say so in the report thread.

## Scope

In scope: anything published in this repository, including its history and its CI logs.

Out of scope: the private platform itself is not available for testing, and nothing here authorizes testing against it or against any product it hosts.
