## Problem

What is wrong or missing today, with the evidence that shows it.

## Desired Outcome

What is true once this is done.

## Scope

What this ticket authorizes.

## Out of Scope

What it does not authorize, even if it looks related.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A specific, checkable condition.

## Verification

How a reviewer confirms each criterion: commands, checks or observations.

## Dependencies / Blockers

Anything that must happen first, or "None".

## Risks / Constraints

What could go wrong, and how the change is rolled back.
